Used for classic Bluetooth RFCOMM/HFP battery reading.

AirPods protocol/state logic is based on AirPodsDesktop
(https://github.com/SpriteOvO/AirPodsDesktop, GPLv3).
The helper reimplements the Apple Continuity Proximity Pairing packet parsing
for local device status display.
